## Visual Effects

Each evolution stage has unique visual effects:

- **Stages 0-3**: Baby forms with soft glows
- **Stages 4-8**: Green Godzilla with increasing glow intensity
- **Stages 9-11**: Burning/Fire forms with pulsing orange/red effects
- **Stages 12-13**: Ice forms with cyan/blue pulsing glow
- **Stages 14-16**: Ultimate forms with purple, rainbow, and golden effects

## How Evolution Works

1. **Gain XP**: Earn XP by winning battles, answering correctly, and being fast
2. **Level Up**: Every 100 XP you gain a level
3. **Evolve**: When you reach the XP threshold, you automatically evolve
4. **Visual Changes**: Your Godzilla sprite changes in both menu and battle screens
5. **Grow Stronger**: Higher stages have larger sprites (up to 2x size)

## XP Sources

- **Victory Bonus**: +50 XP
- **Damage Dealt**: +1 XP per point of damage
- **Accuracy Bonus**: +5 XP per 10% accuracy (max +50 at 100%)
- **Speed Bonus**: +20 XP if average time under 5s, +10 if under 7s

## Tips for Fast Evolution

1. **Select Hard Tables**: 7x, 8x, 9x tables deal more damage = more XP
2. **Answer Quickly**: Speed bonuses give significant XP
3. **Maintain Accuracy**: High accuracy = big XP bonus
4. **Use Special Attacks**: Build combos to unleash Atomic Breath
5. **Play Consistently**: Every battle counts toward your evolution!
