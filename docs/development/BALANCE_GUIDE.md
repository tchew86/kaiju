## Time Penalty System

### How It Works

The new **exponential time penalty** means:
- Fast answers = HIGH rewards
- Slow answers = VERY LOW rewards

### Damage Multiplier by Speed

| Time | Multiplier | Effective Damage |
|------|-----------|------------------|
| 0-1s | 100% | Full damage |
| 2s   | 97%  | Almost full |
| 3s   | 95%  | Slight reduction |
| 4s   | 90%  | Good damage |
| 5s   | 85%  | Moderate reduction |
| 6s   | 77%  | Noticeable penalty |
| 7s   | 65%  | Heavy penalty |
| 8s   | 52%  | Very heavy penalty |
| 9s   | 42%  | Severe penalty |
| 10s  | 35%  | Minimum damage |

### Strategy Tips

**For Maximum Damage:**
1. **Master your tables** - Know them instantly
2. **Answer within 3 seconds** - 95%+ damage
3. **Build combos** - 10+ fast correct = Special Attack
4. **Choose hard tables** - 7x, 8x, 9x have difficulty bonuses

**Speed Thresholds:**
- **Elite (0-3s)**: 95-100% damage → ~25-30 damage per hit
- **Good (4-6s)**: 75-90% damage → ~20-25 damage per hit
- **Average (7-8s)**: 50-70% damage → ~15-20 damage per hit
- **Poor (9-10s)**: 35-50% damage → ~10-15 damage per hit

## XP Calculation

### XP Sources
```
Base XP        = Total damage dealt × 1
Accuracy Bonus = (Accuracy / 10) × 5    [max +50 at 100%]
Speed Bonus    = 20 (if avg < 5s) or 10 (if avg < 7s)
Victory Bonus  = 50 (if you won)
```

## Progression Milestones

### Early Game (Stages 0-5)
- **Duration**: ~8 battles
- **Focus**: Learn basic tables (1-5)
- **Goal**: Reach Juvenile (green Godzilla)

### Mid Game (Stages 6-10)
- **Duration**: ~40 battles
- **Focus**: Master medium tables (6-9)
- **Goal**: Unlock Burning Godzilla (fire form)

### Late Game (Stages 11-13)
- **Duration**: ~60 battles
- **Focus**: Conquer hard tables (10-15)
- **Goal**: Reach Frost Titan (ice form)

### End Game (Stages 14-16)
- **Duration**: ~100 battles
- **Focus**: Perfect all tables (1-20)
- **Goal**: Ascend to God Godzilla (ultimate form)

## Table Difficulty Multipliers

Easy tables deal **less damage** (lower XP):
- **0×, 1×, 10×**: 0.5× difficulty
- **2×, 5×**: 0.8× difficulty

Hard tables deal **more damage** (higher XP):
- **7×, 8×, 9×**: 1.3× difficulty
- Combined with high table numbers (11-20): Up to 1.6× multiplier!

**Best XP Strategy**: Master 7×-9× tables for maximum damage and XP gain!
